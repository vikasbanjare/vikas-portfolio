The project

Lending for small business, made approachable.

Purple Finance lends to micro, small and medium businesses. I designed the whole website. It has to do two jobs at once: reassure a shop owner that a loan is within reach, and give investors and regulators the disclosures they expect.

1
One promise up frontLevelling the playing field, with real borrowers in the hero.
2
Proof in numbersGrowth figures and branch reach sit right under the hero.
3
Two audiences, clear pathsBorrowers get eligibility and benefits. Investors get disclosures in one place.
