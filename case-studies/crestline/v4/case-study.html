Case study · Crestline v4 · Concept

A broking app that never lies about money.

Version 4 follows the master specification: fifteen real-world blind spots, an institutional navy and signal-orange system, three typefaces with one job each, two density modes, honest state machines instead of hard-coded promises, and a 128-screen map.

Summary

Four of the new screens, then the detail.

What v2 got wrong

Lime was illegible as text and read as a gaming app. Ultramarine buttons fought green Buy and red Sell. One heavy font blurred dense tables. Screens promised timings the system could not guarantee.

What v4 does

Navy for calm authority, orange only for focus and deadlines. Manrope for wealth, Inter for UI, JetBrains Mono for data. Wealth and Trader density modes. Live states for orders, pledges, withdrawals and activation.

Read this first

Regulatory details (SEBI rules, margins, tax rates, cut-offs) come from the v4 specification and are dated 2026. Verify every figure with compliance before any real use.

04

State machines

Every money promise is a state the backend confirms, never a sentence on screen.

Smart onboarding router

Welcome and disclosure→Client type→Mobile, OTP, email→PAN and DOB→KRA lookup

Branches

KRA validated→Skip documents (A13)
New, on hold or mismatch→DigiLocker and video KYC→Assisted KYC for accessibility
NRI→FEMA, FATCA, passport route
Bank via ₹1 penny-drop→Offer UPI trading block→Segments and DDPI→Nominees, up to 10→Activation pipeline (A11)

Order that never double-fires

Tap Buy→Pre-checks: stale quote, circuit band, freeze limit, funds→Idempotency key, button locked→Send
Ack under 400 ms→Open, partly filled, filled
Network drop→Reconciling, re-order blocked (SR10)→Poll every 2 s
RMS or exchange reject→Plain reason, one-tap fix (C6R)

MTF and pledge lifecycle

MTF 4x on order pad→Cost drawer: 25% cash, 75% funded, daily interest→Fill→Pledge window 4–7 PM
Pledged→Carry forward, interest accrues
Missed, cash available→Convert to delivery
Missed, no cash→Marked unpaid, may be sold next day
Removed hard-coded promises: 'Credited in 1 working day', 'Tomorrow, 9:15 AM', 'Released in 3 days' and 'You're all set'. Each now shows a live status.

Calculation engine

Exact formulas so engineering never guesses how a number is derived.

Portfolio returns

Unrealised P&L = Σ (Qtyi × LTPi) − Σ (Qtyi × BuyAvgi, FIFO)
XIRR: solve r where Σ Ck / (1 + r)(dk − d0) / 365 = 0

Guardrail: under one year, show absolute return and label XIRR an annualised estimate, so a 2% gain in three days never reads as 1,100%.

Margin and MTF

Daily MTF interest = max(0, Funded − Free ledger credit) × APR / 365
Required cash = 0.50 × (SPAN + Exposure + Expiry ELM)
Cash shortfall = max(0, Required cash − (UPI block used + Cash equivalents))
Equity ratio = (Qty × LTP − Funded) / (Qty × LTP)
Below haircut → shortfall warning · at or below 0.15 → liquidation alert

Bonds

Dirty price = Clean price + Face value × Coupon × Days since last coupon / Days in coupon year
YTM: solve y where Dirty price = Σ CFt / (1 + y)wt + M / (1 + y)wn

Callable bonds above par show yield to call and highlight the lower as yield to worst.

Cost basis after corporate actions

  • Bonus A:B: original lots keep their price. Bonus shares are a new lot at ₹0 dated on the ex-bonus date.
  • Split Fold → Fnew: quantity × Fold/Fnew, price ÷ the same, original dates kept for long-term gains.

Interview and screening

Interview guide (45 minutes)

  • Warm-up. Tell me about how you manage your money day to day.
  • Last time. Tell me about the last time you bought or sold a share or fund. Walk me through it from the start.
  • Before the order. What did you check before you decided? What did you open next?
  • Hesitation. Tell me about a time you stopped partway. What made you stop?
  • Surprise. Tell me about an order that did not go the way you expected. What happened next?
  • Tracking. How do you keep track of how your investments are doing? When did you last look?
  • Help. Tell me about the last time you looked for help. Where did you go?
  • Trust. What makes you feel an app is safe with your money?
  • Wrap-up. Is there anything you wish an app did that none of them do?

Motion and animation

Eight live demos with the exact timing that developers should use. Try them.

A brief tint shows a price moved. Colour plus an arrow, never colour alone.

The line draws left to right when a stock opens. Clip reveal, 700 ms.

Drag the handle to the end to buy. Let go early and it springs back. Press the button below as a keyboard alternative.

A check draws once when an order is filled. 500 ms, then rests.

Rows keep their shape while data loads, so nothing jumps. 1.4 s shimmer, off for reduced motion.

Portfolio value counts to the new figure in 700 ms. Tabular figures stop the width jittering.

The arrow turns as you pull, spins while loading, then settles. 200 ms in, 300 ms out.

Rules

  • Purpose first. Every animation gives feedback, shows continuity or orients the user. None is decoration only.
  • Transform and opacity. Nothing animates layout. The chart reveal uses clip-path, which is paint only.

  • Money is never animated for delight. Order confirmation is a clear state change, not a flourish.
