The brief

One page, three personas.

Design a high-converting homepage for LeadSquared's Healthcare CRM product, aimed at hospital executives, heads of patient experience and marketing directors. Healthcare decision-makers are skeptical, data-driven and risk-averse, so the page had to speak to ROI, compliance and workflow fit at once.

Process

Five stages, brief to build.

01Audience researchThree decision-maker personas: financial executives, IT and compliance, and operations. Each needed a different hook.
02Message hierarchyThree pillars: Financial Impact, Compliance Assurance and EHR Connectivity, ordered by when each objection surfaces.
03WireframingA psychological funnel: Promise, Credibility, Solution, Proof, Differentiation, Action.
04Visual designA clean, light background, chosen over dark mode to signal clarity and precision.
05A/B test proposalControl versus Variant B, with distinct CTA copy to isolate which trust signal drives demo bookings.

Testing strategy

An A/B test proposal for post-launch.

Two variants isolate which trust signal drives the highest demo booking rate.

Control A

  • Headline: 5X Conversions with the Healthcare CRM Built for Growth.
  • CTA: Book a Demo
  • ROI-driven messaging for executive buyers.

Variant B

  • Headline: The HIPAA-Compliant CRM for Seamless Patient Experience.
  • CTA: Schedule Your Consultation
  • Safety and trust messaging for compliance-first buyers.

What was rejected

  • A lead form in the hero: too much friction before self-qualification.
  • A dark, heavy blue theme: light reads as trustworthy in healthcare.
