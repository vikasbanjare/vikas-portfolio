Behind the concept

What changed, and why.

I kept LeadSquared’s font and colours, kept the page light and calm, and showed the product working for one family in place of a wall of features. I set the brief and direction; the page was built with AI assistance and reviewed by me.

Brand, from their site

Inter, blue #0047FF on pill buttons, the violet-to-sky gradient in charts, and purple tags on white cards. Their own site opens dark; I chose light here.

Ideas from Mobbin

Altitude for the airy skies, centre-split headline, image panels with floating cards, arcs and large checklist. Ease for how a healthcare journey can be shown as connected steps.

What is not real yet

The screens are my mock-ups, and the patients and numbers are invented. Features come from LeadSquared’s site and healthcare articles; consent, roles and audit need their team to confirm, and compliance wording needs legal review. The skies are generated in code, not photographs.

How I would test it

This page against a conventional control, measured on demo bookings by role, plus how far people scroll.
