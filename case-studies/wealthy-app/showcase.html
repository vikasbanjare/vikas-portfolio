Problem and solution

Dense interface, clearer paths.

As features grew, the app felt dense. Distributors struggled to scan financial data and reach important actions.

Before

  • Dense, feature-heavy screens
  • Hard to scan financial data
  • Important actions buried

After

  • Clear functional modules
  • Strong visual hierarchy
  • Key metrics and quick actions surfaced
