Leagues

A little friendly competition.

Titan and Champion leagues give each week a goal, with your own row picked out so you can find it at once.

Culture

More than vocabulary.

“Did you know?” cards and polite-form notes teach the culture around the words, like when to say お嬢さん.

Progress

Everything you’ve learned, in pictures.

The Words learned library pairs each word with an illustration, and learners can change course or level at any time.
