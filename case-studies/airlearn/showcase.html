The idea

Learning is a streak. Design it like one.

Language apps lose people in week two. This UI gives every day a place on a visible path, a reward to collect and a friendly guide to walk with. The interface stays light and airy so the playful 3D elements do the talking.

01

Home: the daily quest

  • Week strip with a clear "today". A navy pill marks the current day, so orientation takes no thought.
  • Progress in one glance. An amber bar and a 30% bubble show how much of today is done.
  • Isometric day bridge. The mascot stands on the current tile with a speech bubble, and the next days wait ahead.
  • Floating pill tab bar. Home, Tasks, Rewards and Settings, with amber marking the active tab.
HierarchyProgressIllustration
02

Rewards: a reason to return

  • Eight-tile reward grid. The active day glows amber against calm blue tiles, so the state is obvious.
  • One loud action. A glossy green "Collect Reward" button is the clear next step.
  • Badges worth showing off. Hexagon trophies in blue, green and red give each skill its own colour.
  • Warm, plain copy. Short lines like "Collect your daily reward" keep the tone friendly.
GamificationStatesBadges
03

Daily Missions: small wins

  • Mascot hero. The cat waves from the clouds over a soft sky, a calm start to the screen.
  • Level and chips. A green level bar sits above streak, rewards and rank chips for quick status.
  • Ring progress for habits. Water, sleep, steps and quizzes each get their own colour and live value.
  • Exercises below the fold. Lessons show chapters, a progress bar and a completion tick.
